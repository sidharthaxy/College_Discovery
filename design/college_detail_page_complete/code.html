About IIT Delhi

Established in 1961, the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi (IITD) is one of the premier public technical and research universities located in Hauz Khas, Delhi. It was designated as an Institute of National Importance by the IIT Act of 1961 and was later granted the status of Institute of Eminence in 2018.

The campus extends over an area of 320 acres and is surrounded by beautiful monuments and landscapes. IIT Delhi is known for its world-class research facilities, highly qualified faculty, and an extremely competitive student environment that fosters innovation and technical excellence across all engineering disciplines.

With a strong emphasis on interdisciplinary research and industry collaboration, the institute consistently ranks among the top engineering colleges globally. It offers a vibrant ecosystem for startups through its Science and Technology Park (Scitech Park) and various incubation centers.
